# Audio lectures — Neville in his own voice

There are no authentic Neville Goddard *videos* of the lectures; what remains are **tape and radio recordings** of him speaking. Many modern Spotify / YouTube “Neville podcasts” are **AI voices or paraphrases**. Prefer the sources below: original audio, publicly archived, and widely cited by long-running community collections.

> **How to listen:** headphones help — these are mid-century tapes, often with room noise. That grain is part of the real transmission.

## Spotify / podcasts — use with care

Neville did **not** leave an official Spotify catalog. Community podcasts and playlists exist; many are AI or modern narration.

| Type | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Search tip** | On Spotify / Apple Podcasts / YouTube Music, look for titles that say **“original audio”**, **“his voice”**, **“tape”**, or **“NO AI”**. Skip anything that sounds like a modern podcast host or synthetic voice. |
| **Community playlist (fan-curated)** | Example fan playlist discussed on r/NevilleGoddard while building a complete lecture set: [Spotify playlist 7DBQb1lVKktR5Cv6gS5d8a](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/7DBQb1lVKktR5Cv6gS5d8a) — verify episode-by-episode that it is **his voice**, not AI. |
| **Books read aloud** | Separate from lecture tapes: e.g. Audible *Neville Goddard: The Complete Reader* is a **narrated book set**, not Neville speaking. Useful, but not “original lecture audio.” |
